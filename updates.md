# What's New in Inskea

## v3.1.1
### Fast Image Rendering
- Blob URL caching: In-memory Blob URLs and asynchronous background decoding eliminate canvas lag and UI freezes during image operations.
- Dynamic raster downscaling: High-resolution images are intelligently downscaled to match the active viewport and zoom level for fluid panning and zooming.

### Seamless SKEA Drop & Non-Destructive Imports
- Non-destructive import: Drag and drop `.skea` files directly onto any active canvas to merge drawings seamlessly without overwriting your current work.
- Smart clipboard paste: Paste images and screenshots directly from the clipboard onto the canvas.

### Centered Collection Placement
- Collection placement: When adding snippets from either local or global catalogue libraries, snippets are centered directly under the cursor and track mouse movement until clicked onto the canvas.
- Smooth edge following: Canvas screen edge panning continues to follow cursor movement while positioning snippets.

### Refined Export Scopes & Document Naming
- Flexible export scopes: Export using Full Canvas, Current Viewport, Selected Elements, or Custom Crop Region.
- Improved document naming: Exports and window titles accurately reflect the active file name.

## v3.1.0
### Reusable Snippet Catalogue
- Component library: Save your drawings or shape selections to local document or global libraries.
- Starred snippets: Pin your favorite snippets to the top for rapid access across any drawing.

### Frame Navigation & Presentation Mode
- Frame grouping: Organize sections of your infinite canvas into structured slide frames.
- Presentation reader: Step through frames in sequence with keyboard shortcuts and fullscreen presentation mode.

## v3.0.0
### Infinite Canvas & Vector Engine
- High-performance infinite 2D canvas with smart guides, snapping, and undo/redo history.
- Vector shape tools: Rectangles, ellipses, straight and curved lines, arrows, freehand drawing, and rich text.
- Full offline support, autosave persistence, and dark/light themes.
